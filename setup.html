      What to do with it
      
Before you start: click Use this template on the
      GitHub repo to get your own copy. Everything below happens in your copy, not the original.

      
    
        
  1. Open config.js in your repo, delete everything in it, paste the block above. Commit.
    2. 
        
  2. Paste the meta tags over the matching <meta> lines in index.html. Commit.
    3. 
        
  3. Replace icon.png with your own square logo (it becomes the favicon and the app icon).
    4. 
        
  4. Settings → Pages → deploy from main, root. Your site is now live.
    5. 
        
  5. Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → add a variable named
        SHEET_CSV_URL, with the CSV link shown in your config above.
    6. 
        
  6. Actions → "Sync catalogue data" → Run workflow. From then on it runs itself nightly.
    7. 
        
  7. Print a QR code pointing at your site. That is the entire deployment.
